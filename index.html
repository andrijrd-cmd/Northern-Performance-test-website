About

Northern Performance is a results-driven rehab and wellness practice built for athletes, active individuals, and anyone ready to reclaim their body's potential. My evidence-based approach combines registered massage therapy, kinesiology, and rehabilitation science to restore function and elevate performance.

I believe recovery and peak performance are not separate goals — they are the same journey. I work with you to build a plan grounded in your body, your goals, and the terrain you're navigating. No guesswork, no filler — just purposeful, effective care that moves you forward.
